Overview

About the Game

Welcome to Floaty Bird Reverse! Ready to defy gravity?

Think you’ve mastered the classic tapping bird games? Think again! Floaty Bird Reverse takes the classic arcade formula you know and love and flips it completely upside down — literally!

Take control of your blue buddy and navigate through an endless obstacle course of sturdy wooden pipes. But here is the catch: gravity isn’t always what it seems.

Your high scores aren’t just for bragging rights. Earning points gives you coins, and the harder the mode you play, the more coins you get. Challenge yourself to beat your own personal records across all three gravity-defying modes.

Are you ready to become the ultimate Float-master?

Master the Physics

3 Thrilling Game Modes

Mode

Normal

Classic, nostalgic flappy action. Gravity pulls you down, you tap to fly up. Keep it steady and rack up those points!

Mode

Reverse

The floor is the sky! In this mind-bending mode, gravity is completely inverted. You fall upwards, and tapping pushes you downwards. It’s brain-bending fun!

Mode

Switch Gravity

The ultimate test of reflexes! Every single time you pass through a pipe, gravity flips. Up becomes down, and down becomes up. Good luck keeping track!

Guide

How to Play

1

Tap to flap

Tap anywhere on your screen to make the bird flap its wings and stay aloft.

2

Pick your gravity

Choose Normal, Reverse or Switch Gravity from the main menu. Each one bends the rules differently.

3

Watch out for the wood

Those wooden pipes are sturdy. One bonk and it’s game over. Navigate through the gaps to rack up points.

4

Earn coins, unlock birds

You earn coins by playing. The harder the mode, the more coins you get per point. Spend them in the Main Menu shop.

5

Beat your high score

Your best efforts are saved automatically. Can you beat your own record in every mode?
