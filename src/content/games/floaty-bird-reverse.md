Welcome to Floaty Bird Reverse! Ready to defy gravity?

Think you’ve mastered the classic tapping bird games? Think again! **Floaty Bird Reverse** takes the classic arcade formula you know and love and flips it completely upside down — literally!

Take control of your blue buddy and navigate through an endless obstacle course of sturdy wooden pipes. But here is the catch: gravity isn’t always what it seems.

Your high scores aren’t just for bragging rights. Earning points gives you coins, and the harder the mode you play, the more coins you get. Challenge yourself to beat your own personal records across all three gravity-defying modes.

Are you ready to become the ultimate Float-master?
